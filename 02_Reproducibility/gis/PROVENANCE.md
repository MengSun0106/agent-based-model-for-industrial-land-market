# GIS provenance

## Administrative-boundary source

The administrative-boundary layer is based on the 2013 year-end prefecture- and county-level vector data in CTAmap (China Temporal Administrative Map), published by Rui Cheng / 锐多宝. The source page states that the 2013 boundary snapshot is dated 31 December 2013 and was reconstructed using administrative changes from 2014.

- Dataset page: <https://www.shengshixian.com/pages/2013shengshixian/>
- CTAmap download and citation page: <https://www.shengshixian.com/pages/d35ae5/>
- Suggested dataset citation listed by the publisher: Rui Cheng, *CTAmap: A Long-term Series of Provincial, Municipal and County Administrative Division Dataset (2000–2024)*, version 1.30, online dataset, 2025, <http://www.shengshixian.com>.

The GIS layer distributed with this model contains the 11 Zhejiang prefecture-level city polygons and has been modified/enriched for model use. Its attribute table includes model inputs in addition to the administrative-boundary attributes.
